# Licence of the concept labels

The concept labels in this repository (`labels/`, `c1_concepts/concepts_*.csv`, `c1_concepts/concept_mask_*.csv`,
`c1_concepts/excluded_cells.csv`, `c1_concepts/validation/gold_*.txt`) are released under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may share and adapt them for any purpose if you give appropriate credit. Please cite:
1. the paper (see `CITATION.cff`), and
2. the source dataset: R. Khaled et al., "Categorized contrast enhanced mammography dataset for diagnostic and
   artificial intelligence research", *Scientific Data* 9:122 (2022); The Cancer Imaging Archive,
   DOI 10.7937/29kw-ae92 (CC BY 4.0).

The labels are derived from the radiology reports of CDD-CESM. They record what the radiologists wrote, not
an independent reading of the images, and they are not intended for clinical use.
